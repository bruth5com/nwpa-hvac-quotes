Heat Pump Installation for Year-Round Comfort

A heat pump handles both heating and cooling in one system, which makes it an efficient option for homeowners looking to replace an aging furnace and AC at the same time. We connect you with local contractors experienced in sizing and installing heat pumps for NW Pennsylvania's climate.

Your contractor will assess whether a heat pump makes sense for your home given our winter temperatures, and whether a hybrid system with backup heat is the better fit.

  • Efficiency and rebate guidance

Common Questions

Do heat pumps work well in Pennsylvania winters?

Modern heat pumps handle moderate cold well, but many homeowners in this area pair them with a backup furnace for the coldest days — your contractor can size a system that fits how cold it actually gets here.

Is a heat pump cheaper to run than a furnace and separate AC?

Often yes, since it combines heating and cooling into one efficient system, though actual savings depend on your home's insulation, current setup, and local energy costs — your contractor can walk through the specifics.
