Common Questions

How do I know if my AC just needs freon or a full repair?

Low refrigerant is usually a symptom of a leak elsewhere in the system, not a standalone fix — a technician can diagnose the actual cause during a service visit rather than just topping it off.

How long does a new AC installation take?

Most standard central AC installations are completed in a single day; ductless mini-split systems can take 1-2 days depending on the number of zones.
