Emergency HVAC Service When You Can't Wait

A no-heat call in January or a no-AC call during a July heat wave isn't something that can sit on a waiting list. We connect you with local contractors who prioritize emergency requests and can typically respond same-day.

Just mark your request as urgent when you fill out the form, and we'll make sure it's flagged for the fastest possible response from a contractor near you.

What's Included

  • Priority scheduling for no-heat and no-AC calls
  • Same-day emergency furnace and AC repair
  • After-hours availability from many contractors in our network
  • Emergency service for both residential and rural properties
  • Temporary fixes to restore heat or cooling while parts are sourced

Common Questions

How fast is emergency response, really?

It varies by contractor availability and weather conditions, but marked-urgent requests are consistently prioritized ahead of routine service calls, and most homeowners hear back the same day.

Is emergency service more expensive?

Some contractors charge an after-hours or emergency rate for nights and weekends — your contractor will tell you upfront before any work begins so there are no surprises.
