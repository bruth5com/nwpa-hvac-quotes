Common Questions

Is it worth converting from oil to gas heat?

It depends on whether natural gas service reaches your property and how your current system is holding up. Your contractor can walk you through the upfront cost versus the long-term savings for your specific home.

How often should an oil furnace be serviced?

Most manufacturers and technicians recommend an annual cleaning and tune-up, ideally in the fall before heating season starts.
